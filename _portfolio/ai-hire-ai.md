A multimodal multi-agent ecosystem that automates recruiting and job-application workflows for both recruiters and candidates. Built for the Amazon Nova AI Hackathon 2026. [GitHub](https://github.com/anhlamtruong/cafair)

* Multi-agent workflows using Amazon Nova and GPT-5.4 Codex
* OpenClaw, a ChatOps control layer (Discord and CLI) for real-time triage, risk assessment, and verification
* "Safe-Stop" checkpoints that keep automated submissions transparent and accurate
* Type-safe backend with Next.js, Hono, and tRPC, with PostgreSQL (Supabase) for execution logs and agent metrics

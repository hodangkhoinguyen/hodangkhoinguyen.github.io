[KitBase](https://kitbase.ucdavis.edu/) is an open-access database for fast-neutron mutant lines of the rice cultivar Kitaake. I designed, built, and maintained it at the Ronald Lab, UC Davis.

* Mutant and gene search, BLAST, seed-stock ordering, and genome data downloads
* Integrated the JBrowse (React.js) genome browser to visualize sequence, annotation, and mutation tracks
* Improved database query performance by 150% through indexing
* Built Python and Perl pipelines to filter, annotate, and analyze raw genomic data
* Described in *Database* (2026): [doi:10.1093/database/baag024](https://doi.org/10.1093/database/baag024)

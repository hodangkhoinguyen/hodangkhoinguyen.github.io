This article presents an expanded analysis of 3,268 fast-neutron-induced mutant lines of the rice cultivar Kitaake, cataloging more than 428,000 mutations, together with phenotypic data for over 2,700 lines. The data is available through [KitBase](https://kitbase.ucdavis.edu/), which I built and maintained.

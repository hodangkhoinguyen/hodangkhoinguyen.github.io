My name is Nguyen Ho. I am a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at George Mason University (GMU), advised by [Prof. ThanhVu Nguyen](https://github.com/nguyenthanhvuh/).
My research interests center on Deep Neural Network Verification to ensure the safety and trustworthiness of AI/ML, currently focusing on generating benchmarks for Deep Neural Network Verifiers.

Before joining GMU, I obtained my Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science from University of California, Davis (UC Davis).
During my undergraduate, I contributed to multiple educational and community-related projects 
including [rvcodec.js](https://luplab.gitlab.io/rvcodecjs/) under the supervision of Professor Porquet,
[Profficient](https://codelabdavis.medium.com/introduction-ddb70fec369a) at CodeLab,
and [Colorflix](https://github.com/hodangkhoinguyen/Colorflix-Backend) & [TaskCollab](https://github.com/hodangkhoinguyen/TaskCollab) at Google Developer Student Club @ UC Davis.
After graduation, I worked as a software developer at Ronald Lab (UC Davis) where I developed tools for rice mutation analysis and built [KitBase](https://kitbase.ucdavis.edu/) to showcase research result.
